Notes:
This pack was made using DOSBox 0.74 vgm mod.

I made this rip on request.


There isn't much special about the game's music
aside from the fact that the game uses
compressed XMIDI files.
One thing I noticed is, although unrelated to
the OPL music version, that the game uses a
General MIDI sound for its sound effects.

Hacking was quite easy - I just edited some
offsets in ad.mus. But I should note that the
game refuses to load the music if the first
offset is higher than the second one, etc.

The song number indicates the song number in
the music file. Song 14 seems to be some sort
of SFX library and likely contains the two
jingles that have no song number notes.
